Sprite Sheet Maker

Sprites

Drop PNG, JPEG or GIF files anywhere, or paste them. Files are ordered by name. Animated GIFs are split into frames in Chrome and Edge. Import sheet cuts one image into frames along a grid. Clear all removes every sprite and the undo history, so it can't be undone.

Grid

Padding is the gap between cells. Leave cell size empty to fit the largest sprite.

Or scroll over the sheet to zoom toward the cursor. Drag with the middle mouse button to pan.

Align all sprites

Snaps every sprite to this point of its cell and clears per-frame nudges.

Arrange

Click a sprite to select it. Drag it onto another sprite to swap them, or near a sprite's left or right edge to insert it there. Double-click to open it in Preview. Esc clears the selection.

Import sprite sheet

Padding is the gap between cells, margin the border around the whole sheet. Changing the columns updates the cell width and the other way round; the same goes for rows and cell height. Frames are read row by row. Trimmed frames are placed in their cells by the Align all sprites anchor.
