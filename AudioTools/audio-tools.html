Selection

Drag on the waveform to select; drag its edges to adjust; Shift+click extends. Double-click selects the region between splits. Times can be typed as 1:23.4, 83.4 or 500ms.

Cut, copy, paste

Split

A split marks a clip boundary without removing any audio. Drag its handle on the ruler to move it, double-click the handle to remove it. Export can save each region as its own file.

Split at silences

Finds the quiet gaps and splits in the middle of each. Handy for cutting a recording of many sounds or lines into separate clips.

Trim silence

Reverse

Plays the selection (or the whole file) backwards. For a reverse-reverb swell, turn on Reverse in Effects › Reverb.

Fade

Fades across the selection. With nothing selected, fades the start or the end of the file.

Volume

Channels

Reverb

Decay is how long the reverb takes to die away (to −60 dB). Damping makes it darker as it fades. Mix 100% is reverb only. The tail rings on past the selection.

Delay

Feedback sets how many repeats you hear. Pitch per repeat makes each one climb or fall (12 st is an octave). Tone darkens the repeats; all the way right is off. Ping-pong needs a stereo file.

Echo

Each echo comes Delay time after the one before, at Decay times its loudness: 50% halves it every time.

Distortion

Runs at 4× the sample rate, so heavy settings stay clean of harsh aliasing (except Bitcrusher and Sample rate reducer, where that grit is the point).

Compressor

Sounds louder than Threshold are turned down by Ratio (4 : 1 lets 1 dB out for every 4 dB over). Auto makeup raises the result back to the original peak, so quiet parts end up louder. Look-ahead catches sudden hits. Ratio all the way right is a limiter.

Speed

With Keep the pitch on, only the timing changes. Off, it speeds up or slows down like a tape: higher and faster, or lower and slower. Typing a length is handy for fitting a sound to an animation.

Pitch

Changes the pitch and keeps the length. For voices, Keep the voice character avoids the chipmunk or giant effect.

Noise reduction

Removes steady noise such as hiss, fan, air conditioning or buzz. For best results, first select a second or so of just the noise and press Learn. Reduction is how far the noise goes down; higher Sensitivity removes more but can make the sound watery. If you hear voice or music in "only what gets removed", lower Sensitivity.

Hum removal

Cuts the electrical hum and its overtones with very narrow notches, leaving everything else. Raise Harmonics if a buzz remains.

Low / high cut

Both on with Below near 300 Hz and Above near 3 kHz gives a telephone or radio sound.
