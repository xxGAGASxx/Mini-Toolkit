    Interpolation

    Smooth

    OKLab blends evenly to the eye; sRGB matches CSS and most engines. Hue path only applies to OKLCH and HSL. Steps cuts the gradient into flat bands, for toon ramps.

    Adjust

    Applied to the whole gradient after blending; stops keep their colors. Double-click a slider to reset it.

    Presets

    Click to apply to the current gradient, Shift+click to add as a new gradient. Saved presets stay in this browser.

    Texture

     

    Width and height are for one gradient. Atlas layouts stack them with the padding in between. Extend edges repeats each gradient's border pixels into the gaps so filtering never bleeds between rows.

    Shape

    Angle 0° runs left to right and turns clockwise. Scale stretches the gradient around its center; below 100% the outside setting shows. Double-click a slider to reset it.

    Quality

    Smooth gradients band in 8 bits: dithering hides it, 16-bit removes it. Without a background fill, transparent stops stay transparent in the PNG.

    Export

    The PNG keeps the gradient settings inside it. Open it here again to keep editing.
